AOS Orwell Showcases Industry Leadership at SAIPEC 2025

AOS Orwell reinforced its commitment to collaboration, technical innovation, local-content development, workplace inclusion and sustainable growth at SAIPEC 2025.

AOS Orwell February 2025 15 min read

The AOS Orwell team at the 2025 Sub-Saharan Africa International Petroleum Exhibition and Conference.

SAIPEC 2025 provided AOS Orwell with a platform to share ideas, demonstrate technical capability and contribute to conversations shaping Africa’s energy future.

Lagos, Nigeria — AOS Orwell made a strong impact at the 2025 Sub-Saharan Africa International Petroleum Exhibition and Conference, reaffirming its commitment to innovation, local-content development and diversity within the energy sector.

The three-day conference and exhibition programme brought together industry leaders, technical specialists, regulators, service companies, technology providers and other key stakeholders from across Africa’s energy industry.

SAIPEC 2025

AOS Orwell used the event to advance collaboration, demonstrate technical capability and strengthen its contribution to sustainable energy-industry growth.

Driving collaboration for industry growth

On the first day of the event, AOS Orwell’s Managing Director, Akeem Ariyo, joined key industry leaders in a high-level panel discussion on the future of energy in Africa.

The discussion explored how collaboration, strategic partnerships and shared expertise can support sustainable growth and help industry participants navigate a changing energy landscape.

The oil and gas sector must embrace greater collaboration and strategic partnerships. By working together and leveraging our collective expertise, we can drive sustainable growth and navigate the evolving energy landscape in Africa.
Akeem Ariyo, Managing Director, AOS Orwell

Showcasing integrated energy solutions

Throughout the conference, AOS Orwell welcomed industry stakeholders to Stand P10, where attendees explored the company’s solutions in well construction, automation and process management.

Several of AOS Orwell’s original-equipment-manufacturer partners were also present at the stand, including Endress+Hauser, ABB, Det-Tronics, KSB and DCI-PBL.

The partners showcased technologies and solutions designed to improve safety, measurement, automation, flow control and operational performance.

Championing local-content development

AOS Orwell played an important role in discussions focused on strengthening local content within Nigeria’s energy industry.

Abimbola Oni, Team Lead for Local Content Regulation, shared insights into the company’s efforts to develop indigenous expertise and strengthen local participation.

At AOS Orwell, we are not merely participating in Nigeria’s energy sector—we are actively helping to shape its future.
Abimbola Oni, Team Lead, Local Content Regulation

AOS Orwell’s local-content initiatives include scholarships, corporate-social-responsibility programmes, workforce development and the prioritisation of local talent, materials and service capability.

These initiatives support indigenous capacity development and create stronger foundations for sustainable energy-industry growth.

Advancing technical knowledge and operational excellence

AOS Orwell also hosted a technical session in collaboration with one of its expert OEM partners.

The session highlighted developments in flow-control equipment and other technologies designed to support improved operational performance.

Technical sessions at the conference created opportunities for specialists to exchange knowledge, discuss practical industry challenges and explore solutions applicable to African operating environments.

Supporting diversity, equity and inclusion

At a dedicated strategy session, Chinwe Iloh, Assistant General Manager for Government Relations, discussed AOS Orwell’s commitment to diversity, equity and inclusion in the workplace.

Creating a culture of inclusiveness is not simply a goal; it is a necessity. At AOS Orwell, we are building an environment where diverse perspectives can thrive, equal opportunities are supported and local talent is empowered to excel.
Chinwe Iloh, AGM, Government Relations

AOS Orwell recognises that diverse perspectives support stronger decision-making, innovation and organisational resilience.

The company remains committed to creating a workplace where employees can contribute meaningfully and access fair opportunities for professional growth.

Recognition for industry commitment

A major highlight of the event was the SAIPEC Dinner and Awards Night.

AOS Orwell received an Appreciation Award in recognition of its continued support and contribution to the growth and success of SAIPEC 2025.

Industry recognition

The award recognised AOS Orwell’s continued engagement, technical contribution and support for the advancement of Africa’s energy industry.

A successful conclusion to SAIPEC 2025

As the conference concluded, AOS Orwell reinforced its commitment to industry advancement through technical sessions, strategic discussions and valuable collaborations.

The final day included an engaging session on control systems and switchgear, providing further insight into technologies shaping modern industrial operations.

AOS Orwell remains committed to powering possibilities, supporting local capability and driving sustainable growth across Africa’s energy sector.
