AOS Orwell Receives Recognition from ABB for Outstanding Contribution in 2024

ABB recognised AOS Orwell for its contribution to the success of the Smart Power and Smart Buildings business, reinforcing a partnership built around innovation, excellence and sustainable growth.

AOS Orwell 3 February 2025 5 min read

AOS Orwell received a Certificate of Recognition during the ABB Electrification Channel Partner Event in Abu Dhabi.

The recognition reflects AOS Orwell’s contribution to ABB’s Smart Power and Smart Buildings business and the strength of the relationship between both organisations.

Abu Dhabi, UAE — AOS Orwell, a leading oilfield-services provider in Nigeria, received a Certificate of Recognition from ABB for its exceptional contribution to the success of ABB’s Smart Power and Smart Buildings business in 2024.

ABB is a global leader in electrical engineering, electrification, automation and digital technologies.

The recognition highlights AOS Orwell’s role in supporting the availability and delivery of ABB technologies and solutions within the Nigerian market.

Certificate of Recognition

ABB recognised AOS Orwell’s contribution to the performance and success of its Smart Power and Smart Buildings business during 2024.

Recognition at the ABB Channel Partner Event

The award was presented during the ABB Electrification Channel Partner Event held in Abu Dhabi.

Members of ABB’s global leadership team attended the event, including Salil Sharma, Channel Sales Director.

The event brought together channel partners and business leaders to review performance, strengthen relationships and explore opportunities for continued growth.

Representing AOS Orwell at the event

Abumenrie Odigie, Deputy Country Director of the Process Management Division, received the award on behalf of AOS Orwell.

He expressed appreciation for the recognition and reaffirmed AOS Orwell’s commitment to strengthening its partnership with ABB.

The recognition represents an important milestone in the continued relationship between both companies.

Process Management Division

AOS Orwell’s Process Management Division supports customers with electrical, automation, instrumentation and integrated industrial technologies.

A partnership built on shared values

Speaking on the achievement, Managing Director Akeem Ariyo emphasised AOS Orwell’s commitment to building strong partnerships that support sustainable growth.

At AOS Orwell, we believe in building long-term, value-driven relationships with our partners. This recognition from ABB is a testament to our shared vision of excellence and innovation.
Akeem Ariyo, Managing Director, AOS Orwell

The partnership between AOS Orwell and ABB combines ABB’s international technology capability with AOS Orwell’s local market knowledge, technical expertise and customer support.

This combination helps customers access internationally proven technologies with dependable local guidance and implementation support.

Supporting ABB Smart Power solutions

ABB Smart Power technologies support the management, protection and distribution of electrical power across buildings, industrial facilities and infrastructure.

Through its relationship with ABB, AOS Orwell supports access to electrical products and systems designed to improve reliability, safety and operational efficiency.

These solutions are relevant across oil and gas, power, manufacturing, commercial facilities and other industrial sectors.

Advancing Smart Buildings capability

Smart Buildings technologies help organisations improve the control, monitoring and efficiency of building systems.

They can support improved energy management, electrical safety, automation and more intelligent facility operations.

AOS Orwell’s local support helps connect customers with appropriate technologies for their operating requirements.

Strengthening industry partnerships

AOS Orwell continues to build relationships with leading original equipment manufacturers across oil and gas, power and industrial markets.

These partnerships give customers access to genuine technologies supported by local technical knowledge, project experience and after-sales assistance.

The ABB recognition reinforces AOS Orwell’s position as a trusted partner for international technology manufacturers operating in Nigeria.

Trusted OEM relationships

AOS Orwell works with international technology manufacturers to provide genuine products, local expertise and dependable support.

Continuing a commitment to excellence and innovation

AOS Orwell continues to set industry benchmarks across oil and gas services, power and industrial solutions.

The company remains focused on delivering technologies and services that improve customer performance, operational efficiency and long-term value.

Recognition from ABB reflects the progress achieved through collaboration and creates a stronger foundation for future opportunities.
