Theme From Compliance to Culture

AOS Orwell has successfully hosted the 13th edition of its annual Safety Day at its Homebase Facility in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, bringing employees together to reaffirm the importance of workplace safety and strengthen the organisation’s commitment to building a proactive safety culture.

Held under the theme “From Compliance to Culture”, this year’s event emphasised the need to move beyond regulatory compliance and make safety an integral part of everyday decisions, behaviours and operations.

Addressing employees at the event, the Managing Director of AOS Orwell, Akeem Ariyo, emphasised the importance of remaining security-conscious both within the workplace and at home. He urged employees to be alert to their surroundings and take responsibility for reporting suspicious activities or potential threats.

Reinforcing this message, he highlighted the catchphrase:

“If you see something, say something, and when you say something, do something.”

Akeem Ariyo, Managing Director, AOS Orwell

This call to action underscored the importance of vigilance, timely reporting and appropriate action in promoting a safer environment for everyone.

Embedding Safety into Organisational Culture

A key highlight was the keynote address delivered by Dr Chris Mitchel Osazuwa, Fellow and Chairman of the Occupational Health and Safety Association, Nigeria, Rivers State Chapter.

His address reinforced the importance of embedding safety principles into organisational practices and fostering a culture where everyone takes responsibility for protecting themselves and others.

Reinforcing Our QHSE Commitment

The event also marked significant milestones in the organisation’s ongoing safety journey, including the signing of its QHSE Culture Commitment, which is a collection of every staff signature that demonstrates a renewed general resolve to uphold quality, health, safety and environmental standards across all operations.

Recognising Safety Excellence

Employees who demonstrated outstanding commitment to maintaining safe working practices were recognised with Safety Awards, celebrating their contributions to strengthening the organisation’s safety culture.

Another highlight was the unveiling of four new Core Value Champions for 2027. Congratulating the newly appointed champions, the Managing Director encouraged them to remain exemplary ambassadors of the values they represent and to consistently demonstrate these values through their conduct, decisions and interactions with colleagues.

Building a Safer Workplace

The 13th edition of AOS Orwell Safety Day reflects the company’s continued commitment to workplace safety, security awareness and employee wellbeing. It also reinforces the belief that a sustainable safety culture is built through shared responsibility, individual accountability and consistent action.

For AOS Orwell, building a safer workplace goes beyond policies and procedures. It requires every individual to remain conscious of their environment, make responsible choices and actively contribute to protecting lives and assets.

Our Commitment

At AOS Orwell, safety is not just what we do. It is how we work.
