AOS Orwell Hosts 2025 Sales & Marketing Retreat to Drive Strategic Growth

The two-day retreat brought AOS Orwell’s Sales and Marketing teams together to evaluate performance, strengthen customer engagement and align priorities for sustainable growth in 2025.

AOS Orwell February 2025 15 min read

Members of AOS Orwell’s Sales and Marketing teams during the company’s 2025 strategic retreat in Lagos.

The retreat created a focused environment for reviewing performance, sharing insights and aligning the company’s commercial teams around a clear roadmap for 2025.

AOS Orwell successfully held its 2025 Sales and Marketing Retreat, a two-day strategic session at Radisson Blu in Lagos.

The retreat provided an opportunity to review the achievements and challenges of 2024 while developing a comprehensive roadmap for the year ahead.

Key members of the Sales and Marketing teams participated in discussions, collaborative brainstorming and forward-looking strategy development.

Strategic alignment

The retreat helped connect commercial objectives, customer priorities and service-delivery capabilities around one coordinated growth strategy.

Reviewing performance and preparing for growth

A central objective of the retreat was to evaluate the company’s commercial performance during 2024.

The teams reviewed successes, challenges, changing customer expectations and the factors influencing demand across AOS Orwell’s markets.

These discussions helped participants identify areas where stronger coordination, improved market intelligence and more deliberate customer engagement could support future growth.

Building stronger collaboration across teams

The retreat brought the Sales and Marketing teams into one shared strategy-development process.

This created space for both teams to exchange insights, compare customer feedback and identify opportunities for more coordinated execution.

Strong alignment between Sales and Marketing supports clearer communication, better opportunity management and a more consistent customer experience.

Innovation, adaptability and customer focus

During the retreat, Managing Director Akeem Ariyo emphasised the importance of innovation, adaptability and customer-centred solutions.

These principles remain important as the oilfield services industry responds to changing customer needs, new technologies and evolving market conditions.

Our goal for 2025 is clear—strengthen our market presence, deepen client relationships and reinforce our reputation for excellence. This retreat has set the foundation for aligning our efforts toward achieving these objectives.
Akeem Ariyo, Managing Director, AOS Orwell

The message reinforced the importance of ensuring that growth remains connected to real customer value and dependable service delivery.

Responding to market trends and new opportunities

The retreat also provided an opportunity to analyse market trends and explore new areas of business growth.

Participants considered how changing industry priorities, customer investment plans and technology requirements could influence demand for AOS Orwell’s services.

These discussions helped the team refine its approach to presenting solutions across well construction, process automation and oilfield-equipment rental.

Customer-centred planning

Market growth depends on understanding the customer’s operational challenge before presenting the most appropriate technical or commercial solution.

Supporting AOS Orwell’s integrated service portfolio

AOS Orwell’s range of services creates opportunities to support customers across different stages of their projects and operations.

The retreat encouraged teams to consider how individual service offerings can be connected into broader, integrated solutions.

This approach allows the company to respond more effectively to customer needs while improving the visibility of its wider capabilities.

Entering 2025 with renewed purpose

Through shared insight and collective goal-setting, the Sales and Marketing teams concluded the retreat with renewed purpose and clearer strategic direction.

The company enters 2025 with an increased focus on disciplined execution, customer relationships, innovation and sustainable commercial growth.

AOS Orwell remains committed to powering possibilities and delivering world-class services to the oil and gas industry.
