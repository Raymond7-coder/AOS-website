AOS Orwell Employee Community Service Visits Compassionate Centre for Physically Challenged Children

Members of the AOS Orwell Employee Community Service team visited the Compassionate Centre in Nkpogu to spend time with the children and provide essential food supplies.

AOS Orwell 22 November 2024 5 min read

Members of the AOS Orwell Employee Community Service team during their visit to the Compassionate Centre in Nkpogu.

Community support is most meaningful when it responds directly to the needs of people and organisations working every day to improve lives.

As part of AOS Orwell’s ongoing commitment to community engagement, members of the Employee Community Service team visited the Compassionate Centre for Physically Challenged Children in Nkpogu on Friday, 22 November 2024.

The visit provided an opportunity for members of the team to spend time with the children, understand the centre’s daily operations and contribute essential supplies to support its work.

Community service

The initiative reflects AOS Orwell’s commitment to supporting organisations that provide care, protection and opportunity to vulnerable members of society.

Supporting a safe and nurturing environment

During the visit, Sister Maurice Anoje, the centre’s lead administrator, expressed her gratitude to the AOS Orwell team for the care and affection shown to the children.

She highlighted the importance of family connections for every child in the centre’s care and explained the role community support plays in sustaining its operations.

Community support plays an important role in helping the centre provide care, stability and a nurturing environment for the children.
Sister Maurice Anoje, Lead Administrator

Understanding the centre’s daily work

Sister Anoje also provided an overview of the centre’s operations and the people it currently supports.

The centre serves 27 children with physical challenges from different parts of Nigeria.

Its work is supported by 15 active staff members, including caregivers and security personnel, who help maintain a safe and supportive environment.

Providing essential food supplies

In support of the Compassionate Centre’s mission, the AOS Orwell Employee Community Service team donated a range of essential food items.

The donation included bags of rice, beans and garri, together with cartons of Milo, noodles, chicken, fish and tin tomatoes.

Gallons of groundnut oil and palm oil were also provided to help meet the centre’s daily feeding requirements.

Items donated

Rice, beans, garri, Milo, noodles, chicken, fish, tin tomatoes, groundnut oil and palm oil.

Employee-led community engagement

AOS Orwell’s Employee Community Service initiative gives members of staff an opportunity to participate directly in activities that support local communities.

These initiatives extend beyond financial contributions. They create opportunities for employees to engage, listen and understand the needs of organisations and people within the communities where AOS Orwell operates.

Continuing to make a meaningful difference

AOS Orwell remains committed to supporting local communities through its Employee Community Service initiatives.

The visit to the Compassionate Centre represents another meaningful step in the company’s efforts to make a positive contribution where it matters most.

Through sustained engagement and practical support, AOS Orwell will continue to work with organisations creating meaningful impact within their communities.
