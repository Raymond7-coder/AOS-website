New Services/O&M

At AOS Orwell, we are big on Service Operations and Maintenance. Part of our Service Business unit offer Operations & Maintenance Services to various customers. Our Service Business and Operations & Maintenance (O&M) functions include duties and labour associated with the daily operations and normal repairs, replacement of parts and structural components, and other activities needed to preserve an asset so that it continues to provide acceptable service levels and achieves its expected life cycle. Operations & Maintenance (O&M) include routine upkeep as well as corrective repair work

Valves & Rotatory Equipment Asset Management

We help you reduce total lifecycle costs by extending the life of your equipment. Our comprehensive services include full-service repair and reliability programs for pumps, related rotating equipment, valves, and instrumentation. Our certified and highly experienced engineers deliver customizable, easy-to-understand programs supported by updated software tools. These solutions are designed to monitor and support your rotating equipment and other critical assets, ensuring 100% uptime, zero downtime, and the elimination of recurrent lost time.

Maintenance Support

Our maintenance services are designed to help you:

  • Ensure good functionality, reliability, and availability of Distributed Control Systems (DCS).
  • Receive priority on-site assistance within the quickest possible and agreed response time.
  • Gain access to routine maintenance and system checks for sustained performance.
  • Monitor system health to support optimization and operational efficiency.
  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date records and documentation for compliance and tracking.

Metering Skid Solutions

We offer specialized Engineering Design, Construction, and Commissioning of field-proven, innovative solutions for custody transfer flow management within the oil and gas industry. Our services include providing optimized and fit-for-purpose technologies tailored to meet your specific metering requirements. With an integrated facility for the fabrication, assembly, and testing of metering skids, we ensure quality and performance at every stage. All our activities are carried out in-country, enabling our clients to participate in every phase of the project—from design reviews to Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT). In addition, we provide certified after-sales services and offer OEM training, ensuring long-term support and seamless system operation.
