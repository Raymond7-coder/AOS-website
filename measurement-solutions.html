03 / Flow Measurement

Flow Meters

High-performance flowmeters for liquids, gases and steam.

05 / Custody Transfer

Metering Skids / LACT Unit

We offer Coriolis and ultrasonic-based custody transfer metering, loading, unloading and LACT units with mass or volume output.

Applications include custody transfers, asset transfers, terminals, depots, refineries, pipelines and process plants.

These systems are designed to minimize downtime, increase availability, support accurate measurement and improve operational efficiency.

06 / Pressure Regulation

Pressure Reduction Metering Skid

In modern industrial process control, accurate and stable pressure measurement is essential for safe and efficient operation.

AOS Orwell pressure reduction systems combine robust design with advanced sensor technologies to deliver reliable and precise pressure reduction and measurement in demanding environments.

For differential pressure measurement, two sensor modules can be integrated with a single transmitter.

In hydrostatic level measurement, the transmitter digitally calculates differential pressure using process pressure and headspace pressure, supporting accurate and continuous measurement under changing process conditions.

08 / Gas Composition Analysis

Gas Chromatograph

Our Gas Chromatograph is an advanced online process analyzer designed to provide continuous, real-time analysis of the composition and quality of natural gas and hydrocarbon streams.

Applications include custody transfer, fiscal metering, process optimization and quality control across oil and gas, petrochemical, refining and LNG facilities.

Chromatographic separation technology measures individual gas components and enables calculation of properties such as heating value, Wobbe Index, relative density, compressibility, molecular weight and energy content.

The analyzers are designed for harsh industrial environments with high analytical accuracy, long-term stability and modular architecture for simplified servicing.

09 / Flare Measurement

Flare Gas Metering

The AOS Orwell-Endress+Hauser / SICK Flare Gas Metering Solution is an ultrasonic flow measurement system designed for continuous monitoring and quantification of flare gas.

Applications span upstream, midstream, downstream, LNG and petrochemical facilities.

The system is designed to measure highly variable flare gas flows, ranging from low purge flows to high-velocity emergency flaring conditions.

It enables operators to quantify flare gas emissions, optimize plant performance, reduce hydrocarbon losses and support environmental reporting.

Ultrasonic transit-time technology provides non-intrusive measurement with no moving parts, supporting minimal maintenance, reliability and long service life.
