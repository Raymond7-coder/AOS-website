01 / Optical flame detection

Flame Detectors

Our flame-detection solutions provide rapid identification of fires by detecting the ultraviolet, infrared or combined radiation signatures produced by an open flame.

Detector technology is selected according to the fuel type, environmental conditions, required detection distance, field of view and potential sources of false alarm. Solutions can be supplied for both general industrial areas and hazardous-area applications.

  • Ultraviolet flame detectors
  • Infrared and multi-spectrum infrared flame detectors
  • Combined ultraviolet and infrared detection
  • Hazardous-area and weather-resistant detector options
  • Integration with fire and gas control panels

Detector selection and positioning should be based on the facility’s fire-risk assessment, potential fuel sources, obstructions and required coverage.

02 / Hazardous gas monitoring

Gas Detectors

Our fixed gas-detection solutions continuously monitor industrial environments for combustible gases, toxic gases and oxygen deficiency or enrichment.

We support point detectors and open-path detection systems using technologies such as catalytic bead, infrared and electrochemical sensing. Detector selection is matched to the target gas, expected concentration range, process environment and required response time.

  • Combustible and hydrocarbon gas detection
  • Toxic gas detection for gases such as hydrogen sulphide and carbon monoxide
  • Oxygen-deficiency and oxygen-enrichment monitoring
  • Point and open-path gas detection
  • Local alarm, control-panel and shutdown integration

Gas-detector location should consider gas density, ventilation, release points, process conditions and the facility’s hazard analysis.

03 / Integrated safety response

Fire & Gas Detection Safety Systems & Suppression Systems

We provide integrated Fire & Gas safety systems that receive signals from field detectors, evaluate alarm conditions and initiate predefined responses through an approved cause-and-effect strategy.

The system can generate audible and visual alarms, communicate with control-room interfaces, initiate equipment shutdown, close isolation valves, stop ventilation systems and activate the appropriate fire-suppression response.

Suppression solutions are selected according to the identified fire hazard, protected area, occupancy and equipment type. Systems may include water-based, foam, gaseous, clean-agent, water-mist, deluge or localized suppression arrangements.

  • Fire and gas control panels
  • Safety logic and cause-and-effect programming
  • Audible alarms, beacons and emergency notification
  • Emergency shutdown and ventilation-system interfaces
  • Deluge, foam, water-mist and gaseous suppression solutions
  • System testing, commissioning and functional verification

Suppression-system selection must be based on the protected hazard and applicable project, fire-safety, environmental and regulatory requirements.

04 / Very early smoke detection

Smoke Detection VESDA

Very Early Smoke Detection Apparatus systems provide continuous air sampling for the early identification of smoke particles, often before smoke becomes visible or conventional detectors enter alarm.

Air is drawn through a network of sampling pipes and analysed by a highly sensitive detector. Multiple alarm levels can be configured to provide early warning, investigation time and staged emergency response.

Aspirating smoke detection is particularly suitable for critical environments where conventional point detectors may be difficult to access, too slow or affected by high airflow.

  • Aspirating smoke-detection systems
  • Engineered sampling-pipe networks
  • Configurable alert, action and fire-alarm thresholds
  • Integration with fire-alarm and building-management systems
  • Applications for control rooms, data rooms, electrical rooms and critical equipment areas
