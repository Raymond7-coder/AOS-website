Onochie Onyetenu

Chief Sales & Marketing Officer

Onochie C. Onyetenu is an astute Oil and Gas Executive with over 30 years of combined world class experience in Tubular Running Services, Business Development, Client Account Management, Contracting, Tender Management, Financing, Procurement, Agency Management, Sales, Operations, Personnel Recruitment, Risk Analysis & Project financing in the Oil & Gas industry, majority of which were at various executive management levels.

He is the founder and former President of Chisida Energy Services limited; He also founded Mafuta Energy Services Limited in 2013 with Mo Momodu.

He has been a key player in the upstream sector of the oil and gas industry in Nigeria and this has earned him various awards. He is highly committed to excellence in whatever he does.

Onochie holds a BSc from Ambrose Alli University, Ekpoma (former Bendel State University) an Executive certificate in Harvard Professional Development Programs, Participant Strategic Leadership from Harvard Extension School, an Executive certificate in Basic Petroleum Engineering Services from Petroskills, Houston Texas and several courses in the oil & gas industry both locally and internationally.

Currently, Onochie serves as the Chief Sales and Marketing Officer for AOS Orwell Ltd, where he oversees all sales and marketing effort at corporate level. He is happily married and blessed with three children.
