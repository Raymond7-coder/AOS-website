Profile

Leadership focused on long-term growth and institutional development.

Azad Shivdasani is the Chairman of the Board of Directors of AOS Orwell, an indigenous oilfield service provider of choice in Nigeria.

Azad has also served as a trustee of the Inlaks Foundation for over twenty-five years. The Foundation provides scholarships to gifted students to continue their higher education at renowned universities including Harvard, MIT and Cambridge.

From 1985 to 1995, he served as a director of the International Institute for Environment and Development and served as its Treasurer from 1988 to 1993.

Under his leadership, AOS Orwell has become a leading name in Nigeria's oilfield servicing sector.
