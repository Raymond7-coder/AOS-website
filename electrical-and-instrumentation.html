LV & MV Switchgear

Our low voltage switchgear is designed for use in a wide range of applications, including residential, commercial, and industrial settings. It includes panel boards, distribution boards, MCCBs, and more. Our low voltage switchgear is built to withstand the toughest conditions and provide reliable performance. From circuit breakers and buses to enclosures, panel boards and switchboards, we offer a full range of safe, reliable solutions for low-voltage electrical distribution applications.

Control Panels

Our control panels are designed for use in a wide range of applications, including motor control, lighting control, and HVAC control. We can customize our control panels to meet your specific requirements, ensuring that you get exactly what you need to meet your electrical needs.

Busbar Trunking and Cable Management

Busbar Trunking System is the is a system of distributing electric power using copper or aluminium busbar with suitable enclosures and good amount of protection to prevent the cables from getting damaged due to foreign bodies. The compact size and flexible design requires limited space and results in the availability of more usable floor area on every floor, adds a touch of elegance to the ambiance, and reduces on-site installation time.
