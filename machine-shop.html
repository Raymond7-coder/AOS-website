Machine Shop and Fabrication

At AOS Orwell, our Machine Shop & Fabrication Services are designed to deliver precision, reliability, and world-class quality to support the oil and gas industry. From manufacturing and refurbishing vital components to providing advanced welding and fabrication solutions, we ensure that every project meets international standards and client expectations. Our services cover a wide range of machining, threading, coupling, and fabrication works that keep operations running efficiently. Whether it’s supporting drilling and completion, handling repairs, or building custom solutions, AOS Orwell remains your trusted partner for performance and innovation.

  • API 7-1 Products and Services
