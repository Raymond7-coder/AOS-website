Optimizing Casing Running Through Automated Tubular Running Systems

In many fields, manual tubular running methods are still being deployed. The operations depend heavily on the use of manually operated equipment with a high exposure to risks on the red- zone. While skilled hands have delivered results for decades, the risks, handling hazards, and casing string not set at planned depths—are no longer necessary in today’s automated era. The results sometimes have a direct bearing on the AFE days and cost - through unplanned failures and Non-productive times (NPT). Non-Productive Time (NPT) has long been the silent drain on project efficiency in the oilfield. And nowhere is this more evident than in casing and tubing running—an operation that sits at the intersection of speed, precision, and safety.

Enter automated tubular running systems

These systems bring intelligent control and consistency to every connection. With Top drive makeup tools, each casing joint is handled with precision. Errors are drastically reduced, safety improves, and the entire operation runs smoother and faster. The connections are monitored real-time with wireless blue tooth technology.The automation doesn't just save time, it transforms the entire casing and tubing process. Less manual interference means fewer accidents. Real-time feedback from torque-turn systems ensures well integrity is maintained from the start translating to minimized downtime and overall optimization of the operations.

At AOS Orwell, our approach to Casing and Tubular Running Services is rooted in this thinking. We deploy cutting-edge automated technologies to deliver speed, accuracy, and above all, reliability—because in today’s oilfield, every connection counts. Here, we don’t just run tubulars—we run operations that drive results. Whether you’re planning your next well or looking to optimize performance, our team is ready to support you with precision-driven CTR solutions.
