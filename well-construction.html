01 / Well intervention

Fishing

We maintain one of the largest inventories of fishing tools in Nigeria and have more than 100 combined years of experience in fishing operations in-country. Our fishing specialists have successfully worked across many oilfields in Nigeria.

During drilling, workover, or production activities, tools, junk, debris, or other foreign objects may become stuck in the well and disrupt operations. Our trained personnel use specialized fishing tools and proven recovery techniques to retrieve these objects, restore well access, and optimize the continued use of the well.

02 / Tubular running

Casing & Tubular Running Services

AOS Orwell is a prominent provider of casing and tubular running systems. We combine conventional and patented casing and tubing running technologies, including Top Casing Drive Systems, to get casing safely to total depth.

Our solutions include casing and tubing running, hammer services, casing accessories, and bucking services. This integrated system creates a fundamentally superior process for getting casing to total depth—on time, every time—while delivering significant improvements in safety, performance, reliability, and efficiency.

03 / Well intervention

Cased Hole Wireline & Slickline Services

We offer a wide range of cased-hole wireline, slickline, and pipe recovery services designed to meet clients’ operational, intervention, and evaluation requirements.

Our experienced and well-trained personnel, supported by our technical alliance partners, are positioned to deliver safe and efficient operations and dependable well-intervention solutions. We bring decades of pipe-recovery and electric-wireline expertise in Nigeria to both offshore and land-based operations.

04 / Well decommissioning

Plug, Abandonment & Slot Recovery

The plug-and-abandonment process involves installing permanent barriers to safely seal an oil well or a section of the well. Where casing retrieval is required for slot recovery or permanent abandonment, a dependable and experienced service partner is essential.

With our broad tool inventory and operational expertise, we can recover casing while reducing intervention time across different installation types. For offshore and deepwater installations, we provide single-trip cutting and recovery solutions that help reduce lengthy intervention periods and associated project costs.

05 / Rental services

Drillpipe Tubular Rentals

Tubulars used in oil and gas production include drill pipes, pup joints, casing, and tubing. Because these assets are exposed to rust, wear, and demanding operating conditions, their maintenance, storage, and logistics can present significant operational challenges.

We provide dependable tubular rental services and a broad range of tubing, drill-string, handling, and ancillary tools. This allows clients to access properly maintained equipment while reducing storage, ownership, and maintenance costs.

06 / Downhole performance

Jar & Downhole Tools

Downhole tools are used throughout oilfield drilling, completion, intervention, and workover activities. We provide a wide range of in-country drilling and downhole tools, supported by automated machine operations that improve efficiency and help reduce project time and cost.

Our equipment is professionally managed and supported by efficient on-site services. We have the capacity to provide specialized tools that improve operational performance and minimize drilling and production costs, including drilling jars, fishing tools, drill pipes, tubular tools, wireline tools, and slickline equipment.

07 / Completion support

Completion, Assembly & Hammer Services

Our completion tools deliver customized and cost-effective solutions that support the well-completion process with dependable service quality and operational performance.

Our hammer services provide an efficient solution for large-diameter tubular driving operations, including conductor and pile driving. The technology can operate at different angles, including horizontally and underwater, without significant energy loss.

For conductor driving, we use high-performance hydraulic hammers capable of driving piles deeper and faster, improving operational efficiency while supporting environmentally responsible project execution.

08 / Well drilling

Directional Drilling And Logging

At AOS Orwell, we understand the need to minimize rig time while delivering a safely and efficiently drilled well. Whether the objective is exploration, appraisal, or development, we focus on more than simply reaching total depth.

We support optimized well placement, formation evaluation, operational safety, and dependable project performance. Through our technical partnerships, we provide high-quality drilling technologies, including drill bits, mud motors, Rotary Steerable Systems, Measurement While Drilling, and Logging While Drilling solutions.

Our personnel are trained to high competency standards and are equipped to deliver reliable results while maintaining strong cost and schedule performance.

09 / Manufacturing and repair

Machine Shop & Fabrication

Our Machine Shop and Fabrication Services deliver precision, reliability, and world-class quality in support of oil and gas operations. From manufacturing and refurbishing critical components to advanced welding and fabrication, every project is executed to recognized international standards.

Our services include machining, threading, coupling, repair, and fabrication activities that help keep client operations running efficiently. Whether supporting drilling and completion, refurbishing damaged equipment, or building custom-engineered solutions, AOS Orwell provides dependable performance and technical innovation.
