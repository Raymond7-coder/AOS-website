01 / Supervisory control

SCADA

Our Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition solutions provide centralized monitoring and control of industrial processes, equipment and geographically distributed assets. We integrate field devices, Remote Terminal Units, control systems, communication networks and operator interfaces into a unified operational platform.

Through real-time visualization, alarm management, historical data collection and remote operational control, our SCADA solutions help operators identify abnormal conditions quickly, improve decision-making and maintain reliable production across both local and remote facilities.

02 / Integrated control and safety

Integrated Control & Safety Systems

We are an in-country partner providing Integrated Control and Safety System solutions for process automation with SIL 3 integrity.

We operate one of Africa’s largest Exida-approved ICSS assembly facilities, enabling us to provide advanced system integration for the oil and gas, power, renewable energy, water and healthcare industries.

Our ICSS solutions provide both process-control and safety functions by operating the Basic Process Control System and Safety Instrumented System on a common platform. The systems can be delivered as a complete integrated package or provided as separate control and safety services according to the project’s operational requirements.

03 / Programmable automation

Programmable Logic Controllers

Our PLC solutions provide reliable and flexible automation for machines, process units, utility systems and packaged equipment. We support PLC system design, hardware selection, control-panel assembly, software development, testing, commissioning and integration with field instrumentation and supervisory systems.

We also support system upgrades, migration of obsolete control platforms, redundant controller architectures, communication interface development and troubleshooting of existing automation systems.

04 / Industrial digitalization

Digital Solutions

Our Digital Solutions integrate industrial automation, field instrumentation and digital technologies to provide real-time visibility into operations and asset performance.

From data integration and remote monitoring to analytics and performance dashboards, we help clients optimize processes, improve reliability, reduce downtime and accelerate their digital transformation.

05 / Environmental performance

Greenhouse Emissions Monitoring

Our Greenhouse Emissions Monitoring solutions help operators accurately measure, monitor and report greenhouse gas emissions to support regulatory compliance, ESG reporting and sustainability goals.

Using advanced monitoring technologies and integrated data solutions, we deliver reliable emissions data that enables clients to improve environmental performance, optimize operations and make informed decisions with confidence.

06 / Operational technology protection

Cybersecurity Solutions

Our industrial cybersecurity solutions help protect operational technology environments, automation systems and critical process infrastructure against unauthorized access, disruption and emerging digital threats.

Our approach can include OT cybersecurity assessments, network segmentation, secure remote access, system hardening, user-access controls, backup and recovery planning, vulnerability management and continuous security monitoring.

By integrating security requirements into the automation lifecycle, we help clients strengthen system resilience while preserving the availability and safety of critical operations.

07 / Pipeline surveillance

Pipeline Leak Detection

Our Pipeline Leak Detection solutions provide continuous monitoring of pipeline operating conditions to support the early identification of leaks, abnormal flow behaviour and potential integrity events.

By integrating pressure, flow, temperature and other available field data with analytical monitoring tools, alarm systems and SCADA platforms, operators can detect possible losses more quickly and coordinate an effective operational response.

The solution can be configured for transmission pipelines, gathering networks and other critical fluid-transfer systems to improve environmental protection, production accountability and pipeline availability.
