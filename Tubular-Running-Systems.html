Optimizing Casing Running Through Automated Tubular Running Systems.

AOS Orwell, a leading indigenous provider of oilfield services and solutions, proudly participated at the 2024 Edition of the Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE) Nigeria Annual International Conference and Exhibition (NAICE). The event, held at Eko Hotel and Suites from August 5 to August 7, 2024, brought together industry leaders, experts, and stakeholders to discuss and showcase the latest advancements in the oil and gas sector.

At the exhibition, AOS Orwell showcased cutting-edge technologies and turnkey project solutions at booths 27 and 29. The showcase highlighted AOS Orwell’s commitment to driving innovation and efficiency in the oil and gas industry, with a focus on delivering comprehensive services that cover the entire lifecycle of oil and gas projects.

In recognition of AOS Orwell’s outstanding showcase, the organizers of the 2024 NAICE awarded the firm first position in the indigenous exhibitors’ category. Per the organizers, AOS Orwell was awarded first position, “for outstanding display of creativity and technical excellence”

Speaking at the event, Mr. Akeem Ariyo, the Managing Director of AOS Orwell, remarked, “Our participation at SPE NAICE 2024 underscores our commitment to advancing the energy sector in Nigeria and beyond. This platform provided us with an invaluable opportunity to engage with our partners, clients, and industry peers, and to demonstrate how our solutions can contribute to optimizing oil and gas operations.”

In a similar vein, Mr. Onochie Onyetenu, The Chief Sales and Marketing Officer at AOS Orwell mentioned that “The event not only enabled us to highlight our innovative solutions but to also anticipate future needs of the market, ensuring we continue to deliver solutions that facilitate sustainable growth for our clients in the oil and gas sector.

Throughout the event, the AOS Orwell team discussed the company’s offerings and explored potential collaborations. The exhibition concluded on Wednesday, August 7, 2024, and AOS Orwell welcomed numerous attendees to their booths to learn more about the company and its offerings.
