Who we are

Empowering the energy industry with integrated expertise

We are an all-inclusive provider of integrated, specialised oilfield products and solutions across the oil and gas value chain.

Our total-package model enables us to support sizeable projects from planning, procurement and supply-chain coordination through engineering, execution, operations and maintenance.

With strong local content, experienced personnel, world-class technology partnerships and extensive operational capability, AOS Orwell provides dependable services across Well Construction, Process Management, Electrical and Instrumentation, and Operations and Maintenance.

Our history

More than four decades of indigenous energy-service excellence

From our incorporation in 1981, AOS Orwell has grown through strategic acquisitions, technology partnerships, local-capacity development and continuous investment in specialised facilities and personnel.

Today, we are one of the foremost indigenous oilfield servicing companies in the country, supporting clients with integrated expertise throughout the asset lifecycle.

OUR MILESTONES

1981

• Incorporation of Africa Oilfield Services.

1991

• Acquisition of fishing and machine shop business from BP/NSCO.

2002

• Orwell Nigerian becomes a privately owned indigenous company.

• Acquisition of wireline & pipe recovery business from Weatherford.

2006

• Launched pressure control business.

2007

• First local assembly of PDC bits.

• Fishing training school.

2011

• Acquisition of NOV Fishing business.

• Acquisition of Equipment and Tooling Africa.

• Merger of Africa Oilfield Services Ltd and Orwell International Nigeria Limited.

2012

• Acquisition of Otiotols Africa Limited & Titan Tubulars Nigeria Limited.

• Built the largest conductor casing and welding fabrication shop.

• Nigerian Content Achievement Award (Oil and Gas Council).

2013

• Deployed ERP system.

• Built first instrumentation cabinet assembly plant.

• Crossed US$100 million revenue mark.

2014

• Launched a valve repair and maintenance centre.

2015

• Launched first metering skid facility.

2017

• Completed the world-class home base facility in Port Harcourt.

2020

• Received the contract award for O&M service from NLNG valued at US$63 million.

→ Continued progress

Our Core Values

At AOS Orwell, our core values represent a set of guiding principles that pilot our people and business and form the support structure upon which we conduct our work to achieve the goals of this organisation.

We have been able to find strength in our diversity and make people feel welcome in an exceptionally safe environment that allows everyone to thrive.

Excellence

We get it right from the start. We keep our promises to deliver on all projects and commitments in a timely manner. We do this by maintaining very high standards of behaviour and operations that result in a world-class quality workforce, products and service delivery.

Honesty and Integrity

Our business is open and transparent, with high regard for fairness and equality, while we remain committed to maintaining high ethical standards in all our dealings.

Customer Focus

We foster quality, strategic and lasting relationships with our customers because we understand their needs and how to meet those needs in a way that transforms them into satisfied, loyal brand advocates.

Innovation

We have stayed successful over the years by deploying cutting-edge technology and transferring knowledge to create value through our products and services. We reward and recognise innovation in an enabling environment.
