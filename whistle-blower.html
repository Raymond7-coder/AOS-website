Whistle Blower Policy

AOS Orwell is committed to an ethical, transparent, and accountable workplace. To ensure a safe and confidential way to speak up, we partner with Whistle Blowers (Pty) Ltd, an independent fraud & ethics hotline provider. Reports made in good faith are taken seriously, investigated promptly, and handled fairly. Retaliation is not tolerated. Anonymity is available.

You may report concerns such as fraud, corruption, bribery, theft, conflicts of interest, human rights violations, harassment, health & safety breaches, or any conduct that undermines our values.
