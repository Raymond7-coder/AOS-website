Whistle Blower Policy

A safe channel for raising concerns.

AOS Orwell is committed to an ethical, transparent and accountable workplace. To provide a safe and confidential way to speak up, we partner with Whistle Blowers (Pty) Ltd, an independent fraud and ethics hotline provider.

Reports made in good faith are taken seriously, investigated promptly and handled fairly. Retaliation is not tolerated, and anonymity is available.

Through this policy, we reaffirm our commitment to conducting business responsibly, fostering trust and ensuring that everyone feels empowered to speak up without fear.
