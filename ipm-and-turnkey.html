03 / Integrated execution

Integrated Project Management (IPM)

Our Integrated Project Management service delivers comprehensive, full-cycle management of well projects through a single, multidisciplinary team. We integrate planning, engineering, procurement, logistics, equipment, service delivery, and operational oversight to provide seamless project execution.

This collaborative approach enhances efficiency, improves decision-making, reduces non-productive time, and optimizes overall well performance from concept to completion.

04 / Full accountability

Turnkey Well Delivery

Our Turnkey Well Delivery service provides complete end-to-end execution of well projects under a single contract with full performance accountability. From initial planning and engineering through procurement, mobilization, drilling, completion, and project close-out, AOS Orwell assumes responsibility for delivering the agreed project outcomes.

This approach minimizes client interfaces, streamlines execution, improves cost certainty, and ensures safe, reliable, and efficient well delivery.
