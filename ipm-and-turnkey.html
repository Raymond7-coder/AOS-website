IPM & Turnkey Well Delivery

During the entire Well Construction process, we provide seamless services that bridge the divide between drilling and completions. Through a series of integrated activities and processes, we combine the right technology, the right people and several years of experience, to provide winning solutions in well construction and management.

Project Management

Our Project Management service provides expert planning, coordination, and execution support throughout the well lifecycle. Led by an experienced Project Delivery Team, we oversee every stage of the project—from planning and scheduling to resource coordination, risk management, and performance monitoring—to ensure wells are delivered safely, efficiently, on time, and within budget while maintaining the highest operational standards.

Bundled Services

Our Bundled Services solution combines multiple well construction and intervention services into a single, flexible service package tailored to each project's requirements. By integrating complementary services under one commercial framework, clients benefit from simplified contract management, improved operational efficiency, reduced interface risks, and optimized project costs without compromising quality or performance.

Integrated Project Management (IPM)

Our Integrated Project Management (IPM) service delivers comprehensive, full-cycle management of well projects through a single, multidisciplinary team. We integrate planning, engineering, procurement, logistics, equipment, service delivery, and operational oversight to provide seamless project execution. This collaborative approach enhances efficiency, improves decision-making, reduces non-productive time (NPT), and optimizes overall well performance from concept to completion.

Turnkey Well Delivery

Our Turnkey Well Delivery service provides complete end-to-end execution of well projects under a single contract with full performance accountability. From initial planning and engineering through procurement, mobilization, drilling, completion, and project close-out, AOS Orwell assumes responsibility for delivering the agreed project outcomes. This approach minimizes client interfaces, streamlines execution, improves cost certainty, and ensures safe, reliable, and efficient well delivery.
