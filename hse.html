Quality, Health, Safety & Environment (QHSE)

Our commitment to quality and safety defines how we work and who we are.

We are very attentive to the needs of the environment and as such we are committed to ensuring the safest practices, procedures and structure.

We remain compliant with all existing Health, Safety and Environmental Laws. These considerations are interwoven into every aspect of our operations and decisions as an organization.

We operate a Quality Health, Safety and Environmental (QHSE) Management System, as a demonstration of our commitment to safeguard the lives of all our employees and third party contractors in the workplace including the protection of the environment.

We constantly monitor and measure compliance to International Standards in order to achieve our set goals and objectives. This ensues continual improvement.

Beyond providing the needed resources to fulfil our QHSE objectives, we promote training and retraining for consistent development of positive QHSE culture.

All our employees and contractors are trained to consistently fulfil their QHSE obligation as an integral part of their job. By so doing, they take responsibility for the health and Safety of themselves and of others.

Quality Service Delivery & HSE Culture

Safety is non-negotiable at AOS Orwell. Our Safety Policy ensures protection of our people, clients, contractors, and partners by:

  • Enforcing a zero-incident mindset across operations.
  • Proactively assessing and managing risks before they cause harm.
  • Ensuring all staff and contractors are trained and competent.
  • Empowering everyone to uphold and speak up for safe practices.

We do not just comply to Standards, Policies and Procedures, it is our culture to deliver jobs Safely.

Safety Vision Statement

“We Are Committed To Life After Work Through Exceptional Safety Leadership.”

We envision a workplace where safety is ingrained in every choice and job, ensuring every person returns home safely, every day.
