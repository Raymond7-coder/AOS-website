01 / Precise process control

Pressure & Flow Control Valves

AOS Orwell offers high-performance Pressure Control Valves and Flow Control Valves designed to deliver precise and reliable process control across demanding industrial applications.

Our Pressure Control Valves maintain stable process pressure, while our Flow Control Valves accurately regulate the flow of liquids, gases, and steam through continuous modulation. Supported by globally recognized OEM partners and our in-country engineering expertise, these solutions provide safe, efficient, and dependable performance.

  • Globe valves, including angle and globe patterns, anti-surge and cryogenic valves
  • Double-offset, triple-offset and OCT zero-leakage butterfly valves
  • Manual and automated choke valves
  • Pneumatic, diaphragm and manual actuators

Applications: Severe-service valve solutions for high-temperature, high-abrasion and cryogenic processes across oil and gas, LNG, power and other process industries.

02 / Emergency isolation

Emergency Shutdown Valves & Blowdown Valves

AOS Orwell provides high-integrity Emergency Shutdown Valves and Blowdown Valves engineered to safeguard personnel, assets, the environment and critical process infrastructure.

Emergency Shutdown Valves deliver rapid and reliable isolation of process equipment during emergency conditions, while Blowdown Valves enable the controlled depressurization of systems to minimize risk and protect plant integrity.

Designed for safety-critical service and supported by our globally recognized OEM partners and local engineering expertise, these solutions provide dependable performance for upstream, midstream, downstream and power-industry applications.

03 / Isolation and regulation

Gate, Globe & Check Valves

We offer a comprehensive range of Gate, Globe and Check Valves designed to provide reliable isolation, flow regulation and backflow prevention across a wide range of industrial applications.

Engineered for durability, safety and long service life, these valves are available in a variety of materials, pressure classes and end connections to meet the requirements of oil and gas, power, petrochemical and other process industries.

  • Gate valves for dependable full-flow isolation
  • Globe valves for accurate throttling and flow regulation
  • Check valves for reliable backflow prevention
  • Multiple body materials, pressure classes and end connections
04 / High-integrity isolation

Trunnion Ball Valves

Our ball-valve portfolio includes Trunnion Mounted Ball Valves and advanced Zero-Leakage Metal-Seated Ball Valves engineered for reliable isolation in the most demanding service conditions.

Designed for superior performance in high-pressure, high-temperature, abrasive and corrosive applications, these valves provide exceptional durability, bubble-tight shutoff and reduced maintenance requirements.

Supported by globally recognized OEM partners and our local engineering expertise, our metal-seated ball valves are backed by an industry-leading four-year warranty, delivering long-term reliability and confidence.

05 / Advanced sealing technology

Double, Triple-Offset & OCT Butterfly Valves

Our comprehensive range of Double-Offset, Triple-Offset and OCT Butterfly Valves is engineered to deliver reliable isolation and control in demanding process applications.

Designed for exceptional sealing performance, durability and operational efficiency, these valves are suitable for high-pressure, high-temperature and severe-service conditions across oil and gas, petrochemical, power and process industries.

Our patented OCT butterfly-valve technology introduces a fourth offset that provides tight shutoff up to Class 4 leakage. This enables the valve to perform both throttling and isolation functions within the same application.

06 / Overpressure protection

High Integrity Pressure Protection Systems

AOS Orwell provides complete High Integrity Pressure Protection System packages designed to protect process facilities from overpressure while eliminating the need for conventional relief systems in suitable applications.

Engineered to meet stringent functional-safety requirements, our HIPPS solutions integrate high-integrity sensors, logic solvers and fast-acting shutdown valves to provide rapid and reliable isolation during abnormal operating conditions.

  • High-integrity pressure sensors and transmitters
  • Certified safety logic solvers
  • Fast-acting shutdown and isolation valves
  • Integrated testing, validation and functional-safety support
