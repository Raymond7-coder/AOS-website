Offshore floating assets

FPSO & Marine Structural Integrity Services

Through our representation of Marine Technical Limits, AOS Orwell delivers hull and marine structural-integrity services for Nigeria’s offshore floating fleet.

Services include risk-based integrity management, digital inspection workplans and reporting, anomaly management, naval architecture, Class support, repair feasibility studies, on-station repairs, specialist hotwork solutions, composite repairs, coating reinstatement and offshore supervision.

The resulting integrity strategies reinforce safe operation, support production and extend the service life of FPSO and FSO assets.
