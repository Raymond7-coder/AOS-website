IPM & Turnkey Well Delivery Services

At AOS Orwell, we provide full Integrated Project Management and Turnkey Well Delivery Services by offering our clients a one-stop solution for delivering cost-efficient wells, safer and faster. Our approach safeguards our clients from the complexities of managing multiple vendors by bundling the entire scope of work from engineering and planning to execution and handover of the project including drilling, completions, interventions, and workover, under one experience, accountable team.

Whether you’re a major operator or marginal field licensee, our in-country capability and performance-driven model gives you the certainty you need to move from planning to production quicker, safer and confidently.

What We Deliver

  • Project Management: Multidisciplinary teams with global and local expertise
  • End-to-end Integrated Project Management for Well Delivery
  • Bundled services: Tubular handling, drilling and more
  • Turnkey Well Execution with full accountability
  • Transparent reporting and real-time performance tracking

Why Clients Choose Us

  • Simplified project delivery: one partner, one contract
  • Reduced risk & NPT through aligned workflows
  • Faster time-to-first-oil and production increase
  • Local execution with global standards
  • Structured funding solution for eligible projects
